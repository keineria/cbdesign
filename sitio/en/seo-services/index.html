Frequently asked questions

What clients usually ask.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is medium-term work. First changes usually show within weeks and stable results take several months, depending on how competitive each search is.

Do you guarantee the first position on Google?

No, and nobody can honestly guarantee it, because Google decides the order. We do commit to a clear plan, measurable work and transparent reports.

Do you do SEO in Spanish for the US Hispanic market?

Yes. We are native Spanish speakers and work in English, so we can plan and write content for both audiences.

Do I need to rebuild my website to do SEO?

Not always. We audit the current site first. If its structure or speed gets in the way, we explain what is worth fixing or rebuilding.
