Frequently asked questions

What clients usually ask.

What kind of software do you build?

Custom web applications: inventory control, logistics and shipping labels, client portals, registration and payment systems, and automations between platforms.

Can it connect to the systems we already use?

In most cases, yes. We integrate through APIs with online stores, ERPs, CRMs and payment gateways, and confirm it during discovery.

Who owns the software when the project ends?

Ownership and licensing terms are defined in writing in the proposal, before the project starts.

How is the cost defined?

From the scope documented in discovery. We work in stages so each delivery has a clear price and date.
