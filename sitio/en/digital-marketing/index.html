Frequently asked questions

What clients usually ask.

How much should I spend on advertising?

It depends on your industry and goals. We recommend starting with a test budget, measuring cost per lead and scaling only what works.

What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads?

Ads give immediate visibility while you pay for each click. SEO takes longer but brings visits with no cost per click. Combining both usually works best.

Do I need a website to run campaigns?

Yes, or at least a landing page. Without a fast, clear page, a large part of the ad budget is wasted.

How will I know if a campaign is working?

We set up tracking before launch and send reports with spend, leads generated and cost per lead.
